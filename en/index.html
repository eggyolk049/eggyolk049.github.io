About the names
Chroia (χροιά) is Ancient Greek for “the hue that dwells in things.” The name comes from a wish that each person’s own hue may come to dwell in their everyday tools.
Oiko (οἶκος) is Ancient Greek for “house, household.” We chose it as the name of our household account book.
